Phase 1: Under Pressure — Understanding the Inner Load

The Mission

The Mission: Your Wellness Counter (YWC) is a student-led wellness platform that bridges the link between psychology and daily living. This event, "Under Pressure," is designed to help you decouple your worth from your productivity. Our goal is to move from self-blame to self-observation, identifying the "Inner Load" we carry before we attempt to fix our external habits.

Ethical Guardrails & Disclaimer

  • Not a Clinical Substitute: This audit and the accompanying X Space dialogue are for educational and self-reflective purposes only. They do not constitute therapy or clinical diagnosis.
  • Your Safety First: Self-reflection can sometimes bring up difficult emotions. If at any point you feel overwhelmed, please stop the audit, take a break, and prioritize your immediate safety.
  • Privacy: This is a private workbook. You will never be asked to share your answers publicly. This is a space for radical honesty between you and yourself.
  • Professional Support: If you are in a crisis or require professional mental health support, please contact a licensed psychologist or a mental health emergency transition service in your area.

The Audit: Top 8 Reflective Questions

Part 1: The Mirror (Current Capacity)

If I stopped "performing" success or stability for 24 hours, what is the very first thing my body or mind would demand?

What are three things currently on my "plate" that I didn't actually choose for myself, but I feel obligated to carry?

Part 2: The Body (Locating the Stress)

When I check my bank balance or look at my to-do list, where in my body do I feel it first? (e.g., tight jaw, heavy chest, shallow breathing, restless legs).

What is my body’s specific "warning light" that tells me I have moved from "working hard" to "operating on empty"?

Part 3: The Roots (The Survival Story)

What is one unspoken rule about "duty" or "working hard" that I learned from my family?

When the pressure in Nigeria gets too loud, what is my default survival mode?

Part 4: The Internal Cost (The Decision Fog)

How much of my current stress is caused by trying to maintain an identity (or a lifestyle) that I can't actually afford—either financially or emotionally?

What is one specific choice I made recently that I know was driven by "exhaustion" or "burnout" rather than logic?
